NuGet-referenced libraries (PicoGK, Clipper2) are not stored in this repository.

* **Do not copy or link SU2, Gmsh, or MPICH into the repository or binary.** Gmsh is GPL-licensed; invoking it as a standalone external process avoids copyleft propagation to the calling program.
* Simulation outputs (numbers, plots, STL/SVG files) generated by the program belong to the user; the licenses listed above do not claim ownership of user-generated outputs.
